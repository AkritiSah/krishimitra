Goal: have a working development environment and a clearly bounded project, so later phases don't drift.

Step 1: Define the scope (write it down in a scope.md file)

Crops: pick 6 to 8 vegetables common in Sikkim, for example tomato, cabbage, cauliflower, broccoli, radish, chilli, peas, and potato. A small list keeps your knowledge base manageable.
Topics the bot will cover: organic pest and disease control, composting and vermicompost, bio-fertilizers, seed treatment, crop rotation, sowing and harvesting calendar, soil health.
Out of scope: market prices, weather forecasts, chemical pesticides, medical or legal advice.
Languages: Nepali (Devanagari script) and English. The bot should answer in the language of the question.
Users: small farmers and extension workers in Sikkim.
